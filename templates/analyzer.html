Why GC content matters

GC% is the fraction of the sequence that is guanine or cytosine. GC-rich DNA forms slightly stronger base pairing (3 hydrogen bonds vs 2 for A–T), which is why GC content is often used as a coarse descriptor of a sequence's character. In this application it is a descriptive statistic of the supplied sequence only — it does not imply any biological property.
