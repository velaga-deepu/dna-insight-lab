From DNA to Protein

Experiment 03

What Changed?

Objective: locate every difference between a reference and a sample sequence.

  1. Enter a reference and a sample sequence, or load a demo pair.
  2. Run the experiment to compare them base by base.
  3. Inspect matches, differences, similarity and the mismatch table.
Note: sequence differences alone do not establish a mutation, disease or identity.

Experiment 04

Find the Coding Region

Objective: find open reading frames — ATG…stop regions in the three forward reading frames.

  1. Choose a demo sequence or enter your own DNA.
  2. Run the ORF search across the three forward reading frames.
  3. Inspect each ORF's frame, positions, length, DNA and translated protein.
Note: an ORF is a candidate coding region, not proof of a gene.
