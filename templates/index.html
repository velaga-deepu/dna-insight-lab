What the engine computes

Deterministic Python computation on the exact sequence you supply — every result is explained briefly, with full detail available on request.

  • 01Validation & composition — A/T/G/C counts and percentages, GC% and AT%
  • 02Transformations — reverse complement and DNA→RNA transcription
  • 03Translation — codons, amino acids, stop codons and leftover bases
  • 04ORF detection — three forward reading frames, 1-based positions
  • 05Comparison — reference vs sample with a position-level mismatch table
  • 06Reports — printable lab-style reports saved to the built-in database
